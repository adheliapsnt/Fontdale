Founded with a vision to redefine modern retail, FontDale brings together curated brands, dining, and lifestyle experiences in one refined destination.

FOUNDER

Franz Diego

Franz Diego is the visionary behind FontDale, driven by a passion for redefining modern lifestyle destinations. With a strong focus on innovation, design, and experience, he envisioned a space where people can do more than just shop—where they can connect, explore, and create meaningful moments.

Through his leadership, FontDale was developed as a refined environment that blends architecture, curated brands, and immersive experiences into one seamless destination.
